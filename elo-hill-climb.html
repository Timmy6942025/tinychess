Spacing is commit index, linear. Hover for hash, date, message, delta, cumulative, LOS. The board gained an extra +173.9 ±34.3 at 31be6d3 (paired-fused, 1.8× nps) and about +12 at c0505d0 on-device only. Desktop stayed flat there because the change is bit-exact.

How I built the Elo. I walked the 191 commits in topology order and for each commit looked up its SPRT in tools/results.log and BRAG.md. Only commits with an ACCEPT or KEEP and a measured Elo difference contribute. Rejects are logged but the tree was reverted, so cumulative does not move. Error bars are the ± shown in the log. LOS is the likelihood of superiority. I did not smooth or interpolate. One commit equals one x step, even if two accepts were bundled in one commit, the jump there is the sum.

Why internal sum and Stockfish anchor disagree. Internal deltas are sequential, each versus the prior accepted state at bullet TC. They do not add perfectly to a Stockfish anchor because SPRT deltas are noisy at 2+0.02 and because sequential improvements interfere. The anchor is the truth for absolute strength, the hill is the truth for process. Both are shown. The hill is ruthless either way. Roughly 80% of ideas failed.
