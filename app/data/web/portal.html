How to play

Pick your color and difficulty, then tap a piece and tap a target square (dragging works too). The board plays the bot's moves and both clocks run in real time — 10 minutes each plus an increment.

Playing with friends

One game runs at a time. If someone is already playing you'll see their name and can join the waitlist — you're up as soon as they finish or give up the board. Everyone else watches live.

Good to know

This network has no internet — your phone may complain, that's normal. To stop your phone auto-joining later, choose "forget this network" after playing.
