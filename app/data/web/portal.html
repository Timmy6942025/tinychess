DOG-CHESS

A chess board you play from your phone.
Open the board
or type http://192.168.4.1 in your browser

How to play

Pick a color and a time control, then tap a piece and tap where it goes. Drag works too. While the board thinks you can queue your next move as a premove. The board replies on the other side and both clocks run live.

Playing with friends

One game runs at a time. If someone is playing you see the name and can join the waitlist. You are up as soon as the game ends or the board is given up. Everyone else watches live.

Good to know

This network has no internet, so the phone may warn about that. That is normal. To stop auto-joining later, forget this network after playing.
